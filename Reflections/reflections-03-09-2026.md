## creating an splash screen
- splash screen is the initial introductory display to an app or a videogame
- it generally consist of the brand logo, poster, an welcome message and certain live graphics, ensuring visually that the app is loading
- another unique feature present in lot of this splash screens is the progress bar, as it updates user with how much is the app loaded.
- An essential part of an ideal splash screen should be an brief information of the app version, build system and other essential basics, for the user.
