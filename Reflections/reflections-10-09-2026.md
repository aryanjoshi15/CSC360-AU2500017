
# CSC360 Personal Reflections (17/09/2026)

## Session Abstract

In our class today we worked through the index in the textbook and examined the various topics that will be addressed in the course. The index, at first glance, seemed like a simple list of topics, but it actually gave a pretty good overview of how much practical stuff is in the textbook.

## Textbook Topics Covered

The textbook deals with many different areas in computer science. We have encountered some of the topics such as networking, security, databases and other programming concepts. There are also sections on Linear Geometry and Calculus that go much deeper than the basic concepts.

Linear geometry. Vectors and matrices. Matrix multiplication. Dot and cross products. Eigenvectors and eigenvalues. Homogeneous coordinates. Solving systems of equations that are linear. It illustrates how some of these concepts can be implemented in practice using the Apache Commons Math Library, and is therefore more practical than pure mathematical theory.

## The Practical Side

I found interesting that the textbook does not consider these topics as completely separate mathematical concepts. It connects them to programming and to real application.

## General Reflection

Today’s class was mostly an overview of the textbook, but it helped me get a much better idea of what is ahead in the course. There are way more topics than I thought, from basic programming, to networking and security, to math topics like linear math and multivariable calculus.

The index helped me to see that the course is also quite practical. A lot of the topics are not just about learning definitions or formulas, but knowing where they can actually be used in programming and computer science.
